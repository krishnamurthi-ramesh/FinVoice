# FAQ
- Q: How do I change my registered phone number?
  A: You can change your registered phone number by visiting the nearest branch or through the mobile banking app under "Profile Settings".
- Q: What are the customer service hours?
  A: Phone support is available 24/7. Branch hours are 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday to Saturday (closed on 2nd and 4th Saturdays).
- Q: How do I report a lost card?
  A: Immediately call the emergency helpline at 1-800-123-4567 or block it via the mobile app.
