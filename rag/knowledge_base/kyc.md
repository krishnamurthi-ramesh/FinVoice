# KYC Policy
- All customers must complete Full KYC to open an account.
- Required documents: PAN Card, Aadhar Card, and a recent photograph.
- Video KYC is available for remote onboarding. It requires a stable internet connection and physical presence of the PAN card.
- KYC updates must be done every 3 years for high-risk customers, and every 10 years for low-risk customers.
