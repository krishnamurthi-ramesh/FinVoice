# Loans Policy
- Personal loans are available for amounts up to ₹10,000,000.
- The standard interest rate for personal loans is 12% per annum.
- Home loans have a special interest rate of 8.5% per annum for the first 5 years.
- Late EMI payments incur a 2% penalty fee on the outstanding EMI amount.
- Prepayment of loans is allowed after 6 months without any pre-closure charges.
